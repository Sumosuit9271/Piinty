# Piinty visual redesign brief

A ready-to-use prompt for a full visual refresh of Piinty, written from the point of view of an expert web designer designing for 18-30 year olds. Nothing about how the app works changes — only how it looks.

## The prompt

> Act as a senior product designer known for bold, playful consumer apps aimed at 18-30 year olds — think the energy of Monzo, Duolingo, BeReal and Splitwise, but pub-flavoured. Redesign the entire visual language of Piinty, a pint-tracking app for groups of mates. Keep every existing feature, screen and flow exactly as it is; change only the look and feel.
>
> Tone: cheeky, social, a bit chaotic in a good way. It should feel like a scoreboard between friends, never like a finance app.
>
> Colour: build a dark-first palette — deep near-black stout base, warm amber and golden pint tones as the primary accent, a fresh mint-green for positive actions (adding a pint, marking paid) and a hot coral for the "you owe" states. Everything defined as reusable design tokens so light and dark both work and nothing is hardcoded.
>
> Type: pair a chunky, characterful display face for headings, names and big numbers with a clean, highly legible sans for everything else. Numbers in the matrix should feel like a scoreboard — oversized, tabular, confident.
>
> Surfaces: rounded, soft-edged cards with subtle depth and a faint warm glow on the primary accent. Generous spacing. Thin, low-contrast dividers instead of heavy borders.
>
> Key screens:
> - Sign in / sign up: full-bleed dark background with a soft amber glow behind the logo, oversized logo, minimal fields, one big pill-shaped primary button, the home-screen guide tucked into a quiet dismissible card.
> - Groups list: chat-app style rows with large avatars, group name, and a small badge showing outstanding pints; big floating action button to create a group.
> - Group page: a sticky, compact header; the leaderboard as two hero cards ("Pint Pappy" gold, "Roundling" coral) with large avatars, crown/jester flourishes, and all-time totals in small caps underneath.
> - The matrix: the centrepiece. Sticky first column and header row so names stay visible while scrolling. Bigger avatars, smaller names. Cells show a huge number, "–" when zero, a subtle amber tint that deepens with the count, and two compact pill buttons underneath.
> - Pint history: timeline-style list with photo thumbnails, a satisfying tap-to-fill circle for marking paid, and relative timestamps ("2 days ago").
>
> Motion: quick and springy, never slow. A tiny bounce and count-up when a pint is added, a fill-and-tick animation when one is cleared, gentle stagger as cards enter, and clear pressed states on every tap target. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
>
> Mobile first: designed for a phone held one-handed, with everything important reachable by thumb, then scaled up gracefully for desktop.
>
> Avoid: corporate blues, generic purple gradients, default system fonts, thin grey outlines, and anything that looks like a spreadsheet.
